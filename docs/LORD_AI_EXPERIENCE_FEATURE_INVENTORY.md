## Access and verification limits

- The guest-session route allows `/chat` and `/voice`; guest attempts to reach protected routes return to Chat. This was tested in the browser.
- Authentication landing, Chat, and Voice captures are live. The Experience page labels the product previews with their actual pages.
- Remaining modules were inspected in source to create an accurate inventory, but are not reported as browser-verified.
- The user offered to sign in to a disposable account in the local browser so authenticated screens can be captured. The automated Chromium profile used for this pass is isolated from the user's browser session; protected captures remain pending a shared signed-in context.
- External speech recognition, TTS, image generation, search, smart-home, and device integrations depend on browser permissions or environment configuration and were not verified as working in this session.
- The Experience page uses no stock imagery. Its product screenshots are actual LORD UI captures; module cards without captures are explicitly descriptions, not screenshot substitutes.
